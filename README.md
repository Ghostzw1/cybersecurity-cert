## How to read this portfolio

I keep most of my notes in a physical notebook and participate in the labs offered by the certificate. Two saved Linux note files were available for this update and have been organized and corrected. The remaining notes are newly prepared revision summaries; they are not transcriptions of my notebook.

Individual lab submissions, screenshots, and command outputs have not yet been added. [LABS.md](LABS.md) distinguishes the activities available in the curriculum from work that has a published result. Example commands and SQL queries are reference material, not records of an executed lab.

AI assisted with structuring, drafting, and checking this documentation against the references in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). That assistance is disclosed so readers can assess the study material separately from future hands-on evidence. No assessment grades or certificate award are claimed.
