## Published material is not a lab result

The Linux notes are supported by two saved personal note files. The SQL examples and other summaries were prepared for revision. None supplies the missing observation or result of an individual lab run.

## Turning one activity into a portfolio entry

Copy [the lab record template](templates/LAB-RECORD.md) into the relevant course folder and give it a descriptive name. Include the actual environment, task, steps, result, and lessons learned. Explain a failed attempt if it affected the solution; a reasoned correction is useful evidence.

Use small excerpts of your own outputs that support the conclusion. Remove session tokens, passwords, private addresses, and other identifying details. Do not upload the provider's answer key, a complete course download, or another learner's work. If revisiting a lab, label it as a rerun with the real date.
